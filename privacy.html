Privacy policy

Information we collect

Trip preferences (budget, style, goals) to power destination matching and recommendations; booking details you choose to share or upload; and basic account info if you sign in.

How reasoning stays transparent

Recommendations are generated from your stated preferences, not sold placements. Every suggestion shown in the product comes with the reason behind it, visible to you.

Third parties

For Self-Led trips, flight, hotel, and activity links take you to the relevant booking partner's own site, governed by their own policies once you leave TravelWithMe. For TWM-Led trips, TWM coordinates these bookings on your behalf, working directly with our booking and service partners.
